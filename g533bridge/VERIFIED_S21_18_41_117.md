## Nexmon sources and next gate

- [Upstream #662](https://github.com/seemoo-lab/nexmon/pull/662): merged stock 18.41.117 image, FWID `01-1b4772fd`. **Exact byte match proven above**.
- [Upstream #663](https://github.com/seemoo-lab/nexmon/pull/663): proposed 18.41.117 patch, still open on 2026-10-23. Community S21 monitor-mode success reported 2026-09-28; not tested on this specific handset. Any future patched firmware test must be reversible, have a tested rollback, and be explicitly initiated by the device owner.
- [Existing RX42 read-only private ioctl probe](../bcm4375lab/native/nexprobe.c): queries Broadcom `WLC_GET_MAGIC` 0, `WLC_GET_VERSION` 1, `WLC_GET_MONITOR` 107 and Nexmon capabilities/version commands 400/413/0x600. A successful firmware file match does not mean these runtime ioctls are implemented.

**Do not execute** monitor-mode switching, firmware reload, DFU, flash, or injection as a response to the identity match alone.

## Actual Logitech G533 target

- Logitech G533 headset client A-00072, host receiver A-00073, Avnera proprietary wireless protocol.
- G533 firmware V1.13 host/client was extracted and hashes validated separately.
- FCC grants for *this model* establish 2403.35–2477.35 MHz.
- **The precise G533 modulation, number of channels, hopping sequence, packet format, and pairing messages are still not decoded from the G533-specific firmware or test exhibits.** Other Logitech headset reports mentioning 38 channels or pi/4-DQPSK cannot be substituted as G533-specific proof.
- Wi-Fi `monitor mode` captures 802.11 frames, not arbitrary 2.4 GHz I/Q or proprietary Avnera packets. A patched BCM4375 does not automatically replace the USB A-00073 RF/baseband.

### Next engineering milestone

1. Review patch #663 offline against the now-verified stock image and Samsung driver transport; no installation by default.
2. Independently characterize G533 RF waveform/packet structure using G533-specific RF documentation, firmware reverse engineering, or real captures from RF-capable hardware.
3. Assess whether BCM4375 hardware has a verified non-802.11 baseband or accessible samples permitting Avnera reception and then TX. If not, a software-only phone-as-dongle implementation is not possible with available APIs.
4. Only implement a connection handshake/audio stack after steps 2–3 have actual evidence.
